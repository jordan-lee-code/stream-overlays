# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **Two Truths and a Lie has new names underneath. The game keeps its title.** The overlay now lives in `overlays/spot-the-lie`, so its pages on the website have moved to match and old links stop working. The server is `lie-server.js`, its settings start `LIE_` instead of `TRUTH_`, and its download is `lie-server.zip`. **To update:** rename those on your server, and copy the overlay's new code. Saved votes and scores start afresh.
- **Two Truths and a Lie's server commands are now `!lie`.** `!lie` takes the next step, with `!lienext`, `!lieprev`, `!liereset` and `!lierefresh`. The old names no longer do anything, so if a chatbot runs these for you, rename them there. `!vote` is unchanged.
- Two Truths and a Lie keeps a leaderboard. Each correct answer is a point, and when Well Done ends the card rests on the top five until the next round, and does not leave. Points survive a refresh, and taking a reveal back takes its points with it. `!vote scores clear` or the Clear leaderboard button starts it again, and `!liereset` leaves it alone. It can be switched off, and how many it lists is a setting.
- Two Truths and a Lie praises the people who guessed right. Five seconds after the reveal, the card changes to "Well Done" and their names roll up like end credits, one to a line, then it leaves. That takes 10 seconds however long the list is: the more names, the faster they roll, so the last has always gone before the card does. The card stays the same size. If nobody spotted the lie, it leaves as before. The heading and both timings are settings, and it can be switched off.
- Two Truths and a Lie leaves the screen 10 seconds after the lie is revealed, ready for the next round. It was 12, and **if your widget already has the setting saved**, change Seconds the result stays on screen to 10. It also fixes a card that stayed for good if the overlay was refreshed while a result was showing.
- Two Truths and a Lie puts the three answers on the card. The first `!lie` shows the heading "Name's Answers" over "Spot the lie..", with a statement on each of A, B and C, and opens the vote. They stay up until the second `!lie` lights the lie. Both lines are settings. **Your running order needs `a`, `b` and `c` for each person**, and `lie-server.js` needs updating on your server. An entry without all three behaves as before.
- Be Right Back now vanishes when you type `!brb back`, instead of showing its "Be right back" screen again. It also stays hidden until your first break starts, so if you relied on that screen as a standing card, type `!brb` to show it.
- Be Right Back can hide the clock during a meeting. Untick Show the clock during a meeting to leave just the headline and the rotating lines. It is on by default, and ordinary breaks are not affected.
- The Two Truths and a Lie card now stays one size. It always fills the widget box, and the heading, result tag, rows, lie and footer each keep their place, even while empty, so nothing jumps when a name or a lie appears. Long text shrinks to fit its own space. **Set the card's size with the widget box.** The Grow to fill the box setting is gone, and Size of everything is now Text size, which can only make the text smaller.
- Be Right Back takes a one-off headline from chat, such as `!brb custom "I'm heading out to the shop, see you soon!"`, for when you want to tell chat where you have gone.
- Be Right Back has a meeting mode. `!brb meeting` shows "Currently in a meeting, be back soon <3" as the headline, and takes the same times as `!brb`. The wording is a setting.
- Two Truths and a Lie can run its answer server from chat by itself. Put your key in **Reveals, Secret key** beside the server address, and `!lie`, `!lieprev`, `!lienext`, `!liereset` and `!lierefresh` work from you and your moderators with no chatbot commands to set up. Skips, going back and problems show as a short message on the overlay. If two copies of the overlay hear the same message, the server runs it once. Leave the key empty and everything works as before. **To use it:** update `lie-server.js` on your server and restart it, then remove the five commands from your chatbot. The key is saved with the widget's settings, so take it out before sharing them.
- End Credits now shows its card before the credits roll, with your title held still in the middle, so you can see it and place it without rolling anything. The names still wait for `!credits roll` or the button, and the card comes back when the roll ends or is stopped. **This changes what you see:** if the widget shares an overlay with the things on screen while you are live, untick **Show the card with its title while waiting for the roll** and it stays invisible until you roll, as it did before.
- A new overlay, Two Truths and a Lie. `!vote` opens a vote on A, B and C, and when the lie is revealed the card marks it and says how many people spotted it. Reveal by hand with `!vote reveal B`, or host the small answer server in `overlays/spot-the-lie/server`, which walks through your running order from chat commands and tells the overlay only what has already been read out. Its commands need a secret key, and it comes with steps for hosting it on a Raspberry Pi or another Linux machine behind a Cloudflare Tunnel.
- Socials Bar no longer gets stuck on its first site in Chrome and OBS. On some computers, drifting or twinkling decorations behind the fade made the browser stop redrawing the text, so the bar looked frozen on Instagram on stream while it rotated perfectly well in the StreamElements editor and in Firefox. The widget was moving on to the next site all along, and it was the picture that had stopped. While the sites are rotating, the decorations now stay still, and they move as set whenever the bar is not rotating, so nothing else changes. Thank you to kateooo_ and the person helping with the testing, who tried one setting after another across several computers until the cause showed itself.
- Bits spent through Tangia can reach Mix It Up through a new page, `tangia-relay.html`, that Tangia's Better Alerts can show in its custom iFrame action. Tangia passes the properties of what triggered the alert to the page as query parameters, and the page posts them as JSON to a Mix It Up webhook, adding an `amount` from the likeliest bits or price field. `TangiaBits` now also reads the whole webhook payload as text (`$webhookpayload`), so Mix It Up's JSON mapping, which would not fill in an identifier, is not needed. The page only posts to a Mix It Up webhook address, takes that address after the `#` so it is never sent to the site, and shows nothing unless `?debug=1` is added. Every call to `TangiaBits`, `PressButton` and `Redemption` is now listed on the overlay when its debug setting is on.
- The Mix It Up panel on every dev page is now one reliable path. It downloads a `.miuoverlay` file for Mix It Up's import overlay button, which does not depend on the clipboard, and each widget is checked as it is made: the changed settings read back the same, every starting value and the overlay's own code are in, nothing in the code is a name Mix It Up would replace, and it reads back as JSON. A widget that fails a check is not handed over. The panel lists what is in the widget, setting by setting, and when a StreamElements Data tab is pasted it says which settings it used and names any that this overlay does not have, so nothing is dropped silently. There is also a button for just the Settings property, for a Custom widget made by hand. The site's ready-made widgets are now `<overlay>/<overlay>.miuoverlay`.
- Stream Goals counts bits spent through Blerp. Blerp does not reach StreamElements as a cheer, so the goal reads the message Blerp says in chat, which names the bits ("for 100 bits"). Only Blerp's own account counts, its account name is a setting, and it can be switched off.
- Mix It Up can count bits spent through Tangia, where Tangia's amount can reach it. Running the function `TangiaBits` from a command, with the amount as its parameter (or in an object with a name), counts as bits on every overlay that counts them. Nothing of Tangia's is documented to send that amount, so it needs a webhook into Mix It Up from somewhere, and it is for Mix It Up only: StreamElements has no way to receive it.
- Mix It Up now matches StreamElements more closely. Every editor button is a function a Mix It Up command can run (the Overlay action's Run Widget Function), named as its setting is, with `PressButton` taking the name as a parameter, and the overlay's page lists them. A channel point reward, as Rage Bar reads them, can be sent by running `Redemption` with the reward's title. The overlay's debug setting now shows what Mix It Up sent on the overlay itself, newest first, since a browser source has no console to read. The Mix It Up panel also has the box to paste a StreamElements Data tab into, so the settings come across.
- The troubleshooting import on every dev page now also reads the Fields tab of the StreamElements widget editor (a fields.json), taking each setting's value, where it used to turn one away.
- Streamer.bot is no longer supported by these overlays, and what was added for it is removed: its adapter, the download for it to host, and its choice on the website. It could not be tested on the machine they are made on, and a feature that cannot be tested is not one to keep promising.
- Every overlay now works with Mix It Up as well, as a Custom Overlay Widget. The website has Mix It Up as a third choice at the top, and with it chosen each overlay's page has **Copy the widget**, which packs the overlay's own HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with your settings in a custom property, into the same JSON Mix It Up uses for a widget, and copy buttons for the three code boxes to paste by hand. A new `mixitup.js` defines the functions Mix It Up calls for each event and turns them into the events the overlays already listen for, and the overlays themselves are not changed. Chat is read from the fields of a real Mix It Up chat message, so commands and who may use them work. Mix It Up's documentation does not list the fields of its other events, so those are read by the names they are likely to have, and the overlay's debug setting logs what arrived. Importing the widget is not documented by Mix It Up, so the three code boxes are the fallback.
- Sub Goals, Chat Poll, Rage Bar, Spin Wheel and Squat Tracker now take the height of the StreamElements frame, as the other overlays do, so a panel that was too tall for a short box shrinks to fit it there instead of running past it. Sub Goals also has the two settings Stream Goals has under Look: Shrink to fit the box (on by default, and it was always on before) and Fill the height of the box (on by default, like Stream Goals, so the panel is as tall as the box with its content in the middle; untick it for a panel as tall as its content). Chat Poll keeps its own Grow to fill the box setting. Rage Bar, Spin Wheel and Squat Tracker scale from the box already, so they get the frame height and no new settings. Camera and Subathon already had the frame height and a Shrink to fit setting, and Be Right Back, End Credits and Start Countdown are the whole box on purpose.
- Now Playing: the Scroll speed setting is now Scroll time, the number of seconds a name takes to scroll, pauses at each end included, 12 by default and between 3 and 60. A name takes exactly that time however long it is.
- Now Playing: a name scrolls over the same set time however long it is, about 12 seconds for the whole trip at the usual speed, so a name only just too long drifts calmly instead of zipping back and forth. Only a name so long that it would have to move too fast to read takes longer.
- A new style preset, Text only (no box), for unobtrusive overlays: nothing behind the words, no frame and no shadow, just the text with a soft dark halo so it reads over whatever the stream is showing. It is in the kit and in every overlay, and the same halo applies to any preset or Custom colour set whose panel is fully clear. The overlays that draw their own panel (Chat Poll, Spin Wheel, Squat Tracker, Rage Bar, Subathon and Sub Goals) get the clear panel but not yet the halo or the frame removal.
- Every overlay's dev page has a Troubleshooting import panel. Copy the Data tab of the StreamElements widget editor, paste it in, and the overlay's settings load as they are on stream, so a problem seen there can be looked at again. Stream Goals also takes its own snapshot, with the counts and the time.
- Inside a frame, which is how StreamElements shows a widget, a card now takes the height of the frame, which is the box. The page there is only as tall as its content, so "the height of the box" used to mean "the height of the card", and a card whose text grew went past the box without shrinking to fit, and Fill the height of the box had nothing to fill. This is in the shared theme code, so it reaches every overlay that uses it, and in Subathon's own. Chat Poll, Spin Wheel, Squat Tracker, Rage Bar and Sub Goals do not use the shared shrink to fit, so they are unchanged. The page no longer scrolls inside the frame.
- Stream Goals no longer grows past its box in StreamElements when a goal is reached. The widget there sits in a page only as tall as its content, so "the height of the box" meant "the height of the card", and nothing could fill the box or shrink to fit it. The card now takes its height from the frame it sits in, and each goal's label and count stay on one line, shrinking their text a little instead of wrapping, so a row never gets taller when "Goal reached!" appears. When a reached goal raises itself, its words now fade out and back in with the new goal, instead of snapping.
- The dev page's Troubleshooting snapshot panel also takes the Data tab of the StreamElements editor, which loads the overlay's settings without needing the snapshot button.
- Adding to a goal from chat, such as `!goals add bits 500`, now fills the bar, pops the number, plays the sound and celebrates a reached goal, as a real sub or cheer does, instead of jumping to the new value. It still works while paused, and taking some off stays a quiet correction.
- Stream Goals can show a snapshot of its settings and saved counts, from a button or the `!goals snapshot` chat command, and the dev page has a Troubleshooting snapshot panel that loads one, with the same settings, counts and time, so a problem seen on stream can be looked at again.
- Stream Goals now shows a level next to a goal that raises itself, such as "Sub goal, Level 2", which goes up each time the goal is reached and raised. The wording is a setting, and it can be switched off. Each goal also has a growth setting, so every raise can be a multiple of the one before, such as 1.5 times, and the goals climb faster or slower as you go.
- Card-style panels now fill the height of their box by default, with their content in the middle, so a leaderboard, a task list or a reminder stays where it was put in OBS as entries come and go. Untick Fill the height of the box for the older panel that is as tall as its content.
- A large corner radius no longer lets bars and lines run out past the curve. Every panel now keeps its content inside its rounded corners, which matters most now that a design, radius included, can be carried from one overlay to another.
