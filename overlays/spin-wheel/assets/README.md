# Assets

I bundled no binary assets in here on purpose. The fonts and sounds on your stream should be
yours, chosen and licensed by you, rather than something you quietly inherit from my repo.

## Fonts

Fonts load from Google Fonts at runtime, chosen by the **Font** setting in the Theme group.
Each preset brings its own:

- Cosy cabin (the default) → **Shantell Sans**, soft and hand-drawn
- Cosy summer pastel → **Baloo 2**, rounded and friendly
- Celestial tarot-witchy → **Cinzel**
- Pastel goth → **Quicksand**

Any Google font name works in the Font field, so if none of these feel like your channel, put
your own in and it will just load.

## Sounds

Sounds stay silent until you give them URLs. There are two optional clips, set in the
**Sounds** group:

- **Tick sound**: a short click as the wheel segments pass the pointer.
- **Winner sound**: a small celebration when the winner is revealed.

Paste a direct, publicly reachable URL to a small audio file (mp3 or ogg). For clips you are
free to use, [freesound.org](https://freesound.org) and
[pixabay.com/sound-effects](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/) are both kind places to look;
please read each clip's licence before it goes on stream, since that part is easy to skip and
awkward to undo.

If you would rather host the audio yourself, StreamElements lets you upload it in the widget
editor and hands you back a URL to paste into these fields.
